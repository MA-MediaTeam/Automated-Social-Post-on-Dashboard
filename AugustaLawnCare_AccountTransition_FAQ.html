Frequently Asked Questions

How to move your existing Facebook, Instagram, and Google Business Profile accounts over to your Augusta Lawn Care location.

Should I convert my old accounts or start new ones?

Convert your existing accounts. Updating the name, logo, and details on the accounts you already have keeps everything you've built. Starting over means rebuilding your audience and reviews from zero.

Will I keep my followers and reviews?

Yes, as long as you update your existing accounts instead of creating new ones. Your followers, posts, and reviews stay in place.

On Google, creating a second profile can cause duplicate listings and may separate you from your existing reviews.

What do I need before I start?
Logins for all three accountsYour approved location nameAugusta Lawn Care logoFacebook cover photoBio, website, phone, and emailAnnouncement post
What should my new name be?

Use your approved Augusta Lawn Care location name, such as Augusta Lawn Care of [City], and use it exactly the same way on every platform. Check spelling before you save, since some platforms limit how often you can change it.

How do I change my business name?
Facebook
Edit the Page name in your Page settings. Update the Page username (the @handle in your Page link) there too.
Instagram
Go to Edit profile to change your Name and Username. Your name can only be changed twice in 14 days.
Google Business Profile
Open your existing profile, choose Edit profile, and update the business name. Edit your current profile. Don't create a new one.
How long does a name change take?
Facebook
Facebook may review the change, which can take a few days.
Instagram
Updates right away. Your old username is usually held for 14 days before anyone else can claim it.
Google Business Profile
Google reviews name changes, usually within a few days. Google may also ask you to verify your business again. Complete it quickly so your profile stays fully visible.
What if my name change is rejected?

Platforms are more likely to approve a name change when the new name matches what customers see elsewhere. Update your website, signage, and other listings to the new name first, then submit the change again.

Should I delete my old posts?

No. Archive posts that no longer fit the Augusta Lawn Care brand instead of deleting them. Archived posts can be restored, and keeping your history shows customers your track record. Unpin old posts and refresh your Instagram Highlight covers.

What should my bio say?

Use this template on Facebook and Instagram:

Augusta Lawn Care of [City]
Lawn care, landscaping, and more for [Service Area]
[phone number]
[email]

On Google Business Profile, update your description, website, phone, hours, and service area to match.

How do I let customers know about the change?

Publish an announcement post on all three platforms. Pin it to the top of your Facebook Page and Instagram profile for at least 30 days, and share it to your Instagram Stories.

Can the media team post for me after the switch?

Yes. The Augusta Lawn Care media team offers automated social posting on Facebook, Instagram, and Google Business Profile.
