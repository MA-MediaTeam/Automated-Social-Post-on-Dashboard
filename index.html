Frequently asked questions

How the media team's automated social posting works for your Augusta Lawn Care location.

What is automated social posting?

The Augusta Lawn Care media team creates professionally designed posts for your location and publishes them for you. We handle the content and the schedule so you can focus on running your business.

Where will my posts appear?

On your location's accounts on these platforms:

Facebook Instagram Google Business Profile
What kind of content do you post?
Truck and equipment shots Crew on the job site Before & after photos Seasonal lawn care tips Service highlights 5-star review features On-brand graphics And more!

Everything is created to help local customers find you and choose you.

Do you post videos?

Yes. Your feed will include a mix of photos and videos.

How often will you post?

Three times a week:

Monday Wednesday Friday
Can I still post on my own accounts?

Yes. Your accounts stay yours, and you're welcome to keep sharing your own posts, like photos of completed jobs, team updates, or local promotions. Your own posts work alongside ours.

What are content tracks?

Lawn care looks different depending on where you are, so we offer six climate-based tracks. Each track's content matches the weather and growing season in that region.

Canada
Short growing seasons, spring cleanups, and snow-season services.
Australia
Timed to Southern Hemisphere seasons and Australian lawns.
US Desert
Hot, arid regions. Water-wise landscaping and heat stress.
US Tropical
Hot, humid regions. Warm-season grasses and storm prep.
US Temperate
Four distinct seasons, from spring aeration to fall cleanup.
US Cool
Northern regions. Cool-season grasses and winter prep.
How is my content track selected?

The media team assigns your track based on your location.

What if my track doesn't fit my area?

Some areas fall between climate zones. If your assigned track doesn't match the conditions you see, contact the media team and we'll review it and switch you to a better fit.
