Frequently Asked Questions

How to move your existing accounts over to your Augusta Lawn Care location, and how automated social posting works.

Should I convert my old accounts or start new ones?

Convert your existing accounts. Updating the name, logo, and details on the accounts you already have keeps your followers, posts, and reviews. On Google, creating a second profile can cause duplicate listings and may separate you from your existing reviews.

What do I need before I start?
Logins for all three accountsYour approved location nameAugusta Lawn Care logoFacebook cover photoBio, website, phone, and emailAnnouncement post
How do I change my business name?

Use your approved location name, such as Augusta Lawn Care of [City], exactly the same way on every platform. Check spelling before you save.

Facebook
Edit the Page name and username in your Page settings. Facebook may review the change, which can take a few days.
Instagram
Go to Edit profile to change your Name and Username. Updates right away. Your name can only be changed twice in 14 days.
Google Business Profile
Edit the name on your existing profile. Don't create a new one. Google reviews changes within a few days and may ask you to verify your business again.
What if my name change is rejected?

Approval is more likely when the new name matches what customers see elsewhere. Update your website, signage, and other listings first, then submit the change again.

How should I update my profiles?

Add your new logo and use this bio on Facebook and Instagram:

Augusta Lawn Care of [City]
Lawn care, landscaping, and more for [Service Area]
[phone number]
[email]

On Google Business Profile, update your description, website, phone, hours, and service area to match. Archive old posts that don't fit the brand instead of deleting them.

How do I let customers know about the change?

Publish an announcement post on all three platforms. Pin it to the top of your Facebook Page and Instagram profile for at least 30 days, and share it to your Instagram Stories.

Yes. Your accounts stay yours, and your own posts, like completed jobs, team updates, or local promotions, work alongside ours.

What are content tracks?

Six climate-based tracks that match your content to the weather and growing season in your region.

Canada
Short growing seasons, spring cleanups, and snow-season services.
Australia
Timed to Southern Hemisphere seasons and Australian lawns.
US Desert
Hot, arid regions. Water-wise landscaping and heat stress.
US Tropical
Hot, humid regions. Warm-season grasses and storm prep.
US Temperate
Four distinct seasons, from spring aeration to fall cleanup.
US Cool
Northern regions. Cool-season grasses and winter prep.

The media team assigns your track based on your location. If it doesn't fit your area, contact the media team and we'll switch you.
